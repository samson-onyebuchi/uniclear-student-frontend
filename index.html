ABOUT UNICLEAR

A more organised approach to student clearance

UniClear is designed to bring the student clearance process into one online platform. Students can submit documents for the appropriate clearance stage and check the progress of their submissions.

Authorized staff can review submissions for their assigned stages, approve eligible documents or request corrections where necessary.

  • Centralised clearance information
  • Online document submission
  • Stage-by-stage progress monitoring
  • Role-based access for students and staff
